<section className="ap-section">
  <div className="ap-wrap ap-split">
    <div>
      <p className="ap-kicker">Why</p>
      <h2 className="ap-h2">Agents can do the steps. Something has to hold the process.</h2>
    </div>
    <div className="ap-prose">
      <p>Agents can now screen a supplier, check an invoice or draft a contract summary. What they lack is everything around the steps. Who goes next? Who must sign off? Was the output what was asked for? Who did what, and when?</p>
      <p>Agent Process puts that in a server and keeps the process file small. Everything specific to a scenario stays in plain-language instructions that the agent or person adapts to. The design rule comes from <a href="https://agentskills.io">Agent Skills</a>: <strong>the format carries as little as possible; the instructions and the model carry the rest.</strong> The one exception is the wire between agent and server, where every tool contract is exact.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section className="ap-section">
  <div className="ap-wrap">
    <p className="ap-kicker">What a server guarantees</p>
    <h2 className="ap-h2 ap-narrow">Five rules, enforced on every run. Nothing else.</h2>
    <div className="ap-rules">
      <div className="ap-rule"><span className="ap-num">01</span><h3>Order</h3><p>Work exists only along the declared steps.</p></div>
      <div className="ap-rule"><span className="ap-num">02</span><h3>One actor at a time</h3><p>A claimed step belongs to one agent until it submits, hands off, or its lease ends.</p></div>
      <div className="ap-rule"><span className="ap-num">03</span><h3>Acceptance</h3><p>Output must match the declared fields and evidence, or nothing changes and every issue comes back at once.</p></div>
      <div className="ap-rule"><span className="ap-num">04</span><h3>People decide</h3><p>Only a person completes an approval or a person's task. An agent never can.</p></div>
      <div className="ap-rule"><span className="ap-num">05</span><h3>Immutability</h3><p>A published version never changes, and a run keeps its version for life.</p></div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section className="ap-section">
  <div className="ap-wrap">
    <p className="ap-kicker">How it works</p>
    <h2 className="ap-h2 ap-narrow">From a file to a finished, recorded run.</h2>
    <ol className="ap-flow">
      <li><span>Write</span><p>An author writes <code>PROCESS.md</code>: steps for agents, people's tasks, approvals, waits and finishes, with the fields and evidence each must return.</p></li>
      <li><span>Publish</span><p>A server checks the file, maps each role to a group of people, and publishes a numbered version with a content hash.</p></li>
      <li><span>Work</span><p>Agents call <code>get_work</code>, <code>claim</code> and <code>submit</code> over MCP. People get work items and <code>decide</code>. A stuck agent can <code>escalate</code>.</p></li>
      <li><span>Record</span><p>Every completion keeps who, when, the output, the evidence and a summary. Rework keeps earlier attempts in history.</p></li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</section>
