# Code of conduct

Agent Process follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and considerate, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and keep discussion on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in any project space, including issues, discussions, pull requests and reviews.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature on the comment, issue or discussion concerned. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled in confidence. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation that breaks this code.
